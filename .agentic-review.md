# Review rules for Tessera

`AGENTS.md` states design goals for authors; the bars below decide which departures from them are findings. Cite the owners of acceptance and evidence rules instead of restating them: [performance changes](CONTRIBUTING.md#performance-changes), [validation](CONTRIBUTING.md#validation), [semantics](conformance/semantics.md) and the [engine reference](docs/design/engine.md).

- **Cost of the complete operation.** Judge an added scan, allocation or pass by its effect on the whole call (`sort`, `par_sort`, argsort, cached-key sort, selection) at the sizes in `bench/plans/continuous.toml`, or at the larger workload a roadmap done criterion names, not by key reads in one phase on a constructed input. Bounded parallel reads past a serial early exit are allowed; report them only when the bound is missing, undocumented or exceeded. Once the owner accepts a cost on the PR, do not raise variants of it.
- **Performance evidence.** A slowdown alert in the hosted verdict that the PR neither fixes nor lists as an accepted loss is a finding. Missing local reports, links or placement controls are findings only for a conclusion recorded in engine evidence or a tuned constant credited to the source change.
- **Tests follow the validation rules.** Raise T1 for the contracts the validation section names. Do not request tests that pin internal call counts, key reads, scheduling or worker placement, or tests that duplicate an existing guard.
- **One authority per shared rule.** Judge structure with the [software design philosophy](.agents/skills/software-design-philosophy/SKILL.md) skill. A change that parses or builds an existing format, reads an existing table, applies an existing threshold or identifier rule, or adds a step to an existing pipeline or protocol must route through that rule's current owner. A second implementation beside it, such as a language- or adapter-specific copy of a shared preparation, publication or status path, is a finding that names the owner it bypasses. Differences in an external protocol stay at the adapter edge and are not findings.
- **Root causes, not symptoms.** When several findings share one mechanism (a prefix, special case, flag or extra state), report the mechanism once and prefer a fix that deletes it over one that adds a guard.
- **Squash merges.** Only the PR title is checked against the commit-title rules; commit subjects and how changes are split across commits are not findings.
- **Scope.** Files unrelated to the PR's stated goal get one finding asking to remove them; review their content as usual while they remain.
